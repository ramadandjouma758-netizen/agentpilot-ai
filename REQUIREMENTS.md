# AgentPilot AI — Requirements

## 1. Project Vision

AgentPilot AI is a simple AI-powered commerce platform designed to help online store owners understand their performance, compare themselves with other participating stores, calculate profitability, and access intelligent business assistance.

The product must prioritize simplicity, speed, and clarity.

---

## 2. Core Principles

- Simple user experience.
- Minimal unnecessary data.
- Mobile-friendly interface.
- Clear information hierarchy.
- Fast access to the most important functions.
- Advanced features should remain optional.
- Privacy and user consent are required before displaying store information publicly.

---

## 3. Main User Interface

The product will initially have two primary pages.

### Page 1 — Global Leaders

The first page users see.

It displays the leading participating store from each geographic continent/region based on units sold during the last 24 hours.

The competition is across supported commerce platforms, not separated by platform.

Supported platforms may include:

- Shopify
- Amazon
- Other supported platforms added later

### Leaderboard Information

Each leader should display:

- Rank
- Medal for the top three positions where applicable
- Store name
- Optional owner display name
- Commerce platform
- Geographic region
- Product name
- Product price
- Units sold during the last 24 hours

The interface must remain visually simple.

The system must not expose unnecessary personal information.

Email addresses must not be displayed.

Store owners must have control over whether their store appears publicly.

---

## 4. Geographic Leaderboard

The system should support the following major geographic regions:

- North America
- South America
- Europe
- Africa
- Asia
- Oceania

The system selects one leading participating store for each region.

A store may compete with stores operating on different supported platforms.

Example:

A Shopify store and an Amazon store in Europe compete in the same European leaderboard.

The winner is determined by the highest number of eligible units sold during the defined 24-hour period.

---

## 5. Sales Period

The primary leaderboard period is:

**Last 24 hours**

The system must use a clearly defined time standard and consistently apply it to all participating stores.

Future versions may support:

- 7 days
- 30 days
- Historical rankings

These are not required for the initial MVP.

---

## 6. Profit Calculator

The profit calculator must be a completely independent second page.

Users should reach it from the main interface with a single tap.

The calculator should remain simple by default.

### Basic Inputs

- Selling price
- Product cost
- Advertising cost
- Shipping cost

### Basic Results

- Net profit
- Profit margin

Advanced costs may be hidden under an optional advanced section.

Possible future inputs include:

- Platform fees
- Payment processing fees
- Taxes
- Additional operating costs

The user should not be forced to enter unnecessary information.

---

## 7. AI Assistant

AgentPilot AI is the core intelligent component of the platform.

The AI assistant should eventually help store owners with tasks such as:

- Understanding store performance
- Profitability analysis
- Product analysis
- Business recommendations
- Competitor insights
- Marketing suggestions
- Interpreting store data

AI functionality should be presented simply and progressively.

---

## 8. Free Plan

The free version should provide enough value for users to understand the product.

Potential free features:

- Global leaderboard access
- Basic profit calculator
- Basic store insights
- Limited AI functionality
- Limited usage of advanced tools

Advertising may be displayed in the free version where appropriate.

---

## 9. Paid Plan

The paid version should provide additional value rather than simply removing limitations.

Potential paid features:

- No advertisements
- Advanced analytics
- Advanced profitability analysis
- Store performance comparison
- Competitor insights
- AI business assistance
- Historical data
- Alerts and notifications
- Multiple store connections
- Advanced reporting

Exact pricing will be defined later.

---

## 10. Revenue Model

AgentPilot should not depend entirely on advertising revenue.

Primary potential revenue sources:

1. Paid subscriptions
2. Premium AI features
3. Advanced analytics
4. Multi-store plans
5. Business/agency plans
6. Advertising as a secondary revenue source
7. Potential affiliate or commerce partnerships

The long-term business objective is to build recurring SaaS revenue.

---

## 11. Privacy and Consent

The platform must protect store owners and their data.

Requirements:

- Store data must only be accessed with appropriate authorization.
- Public leaderboard participation should require appropriate consent.
- Personal email addresses must not be publicly displayed.
- Owners should be able to choose an acceptable public display name where applicable.
- Sensitive private store information must never be exposed through the public leaderboard.
- The system must clearly distinguish between publicly displayed information and private analytics.

---

## 12. Data Accuracy

The platform must not claim that its leaderboard represents every store on Amazon, Shopify, or the entire global ecommerce market.

The leaderboard represents participating stores whose data is legitimately available to AgentPilot.

The interface should communicate this clearly when necessary.

---

## 13. MVP Priorities

The first working version should focus on:

1. Simple landing/dashboard interface
2. Global leaderboard
3. Geographic ranking logic
4. Product price display
5. Units sold display
6. Independent profit calculator
7. Basic user accounts
8. Basic store connection architecture
9. Privacy and consent controls
10. Foundation for future AI functionality

Advanced features should be implemented after the core experience works reliably.

---

## 14. Product Design Rule

The main design principle is:

**Simple by default, powerful when needed.**

Users should be able to understand the main screen within seconds.

Do not fill the interface with unnecessary charts, numbers, technical information, or complicated controls.

Advanced information should appear only when the user requests it.

---

## 15. Long-Term Goal

AgentPilot aims to become an intelligent commerce platform that combines:

- Global ecommerce competition
- Profitability tools
- Store analytics
- AI-powered business assistance
- Recurring SaaS subscriptions

The long-term business target is to build a scalable product capable of generating significant recurring monthly revenue.

The target of $100,000+ monthly revenue is a business objective, not a guaranteed outcome.

---

## 16. Future Expansion

Potential future features include:

- More commerce platforms
- More geographic filtering
- Product trend detection
- Historical leaderboards
- Automated alerts
- Advanced competitor intelligence
- AI product recommendations
- AI marketing assistant
- Automated reports
- API access
- Agency dashboards
- Mobile application

These features should not complicate the initial MVP.

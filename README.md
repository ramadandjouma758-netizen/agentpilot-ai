# AgentPilot AI

> Build Your AI Workforce

AgentPilot AI is an AI-powered SaaS platform that enables businesses to create intelligent AI employees capable of answering customer questions, managing company knowledge, automating repetitive tasks, and improving productivity.

---

## Vision

To become one of the world's leading AI Workforce platforms, empowering businesses of all sizes with intelligent digital employees.

---

## Mission

Our mission is to help companies reduce operational costs, automate customer support, and increase productivity through artificial intelligence.

---

## Core Features

- AI Employee Creator
- Knowledge Base
- AI Chat Assistant
- Business Dashboard
- Website Chat Widget
- Analytics & Reports
- User Management
- Subscription System

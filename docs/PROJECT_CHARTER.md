# AgentPilot AI — Project Charter

## 1. Project Overview

AgentPilot AI is an AI Workforce SaaS platform designed to help businesses create, configure, and manage intelligent AI employees.

These AI employees can support customers, use business knowledge, perform defined tasks, and operate according to business rules and permissions.

## 2. Vision

To build a trusted global AI Workforce platform that enables businesses to deploy intelligent digital employees without requiring advanced technical expertise.

## 3. Mission

AgentPilot AI aims to make AI automation accessible to businesses of all sizes by providing simple tools for creating, managing, and deploying reliable AI employees.

## 4. Problem Statement

Businesses spend significant amounts of time and money handling repetitive customer interactions, information requests, internal processes, and routine operational tasks.

Existing AI tools can be powerful but may require technical knowledge, complex configuration, or multiple disconnected services.

AgentPilot AI aims to provide a unified platform where businesses can create and manage AI employees from one workspace.

## 5. Target Users

- Small and medium-sized businesses
- E-commerce businesses
- Startups
- Marketing agencies
- Educational organizations
- Service businesses
- Independent professionals

## 6. Core Product Concept

The core product is an AI Employee.

Each AI Employee should have:

- A defined role
- A personality and communication style
- Business knowledge
- Instructions and policies
- Authorized tools
- Defined tasks
- Permission boundaries
- Performance monitoring

## 7. Initial MVP

The first Minimum Viable Product will focus on:

- User registration and authentication
- Business workspace
- AI Employee creation
- AI Employee configuration
- Knowledge base
- Document upload
- AI conversation
- Website chat widget
- Basic analytics
- Employee and workspace management

## 8. Product Principles

AgentPilot AI will be designed around the following principles:

1. Simplicity
2. Reliability
3. Security
4. Transparency
5. Human control
6. Scalability
7. Excellent user experience

## 9. Technology Direction

The initial technology direction includes:

- Next.js
- React
- Node.js
- PostgreSQL
- AI API integration
- GitHub
- Cloud deployment

Technology choices may evolve during development based on technical and business requirements.

## 10. Development Phases

### Phase 1 — Foundation
Project documentation, repository structure, requirements, and architecture.

### Phase 2 — MVP
Core AI Employee creation, knowledge, conversations, and workspace management.

### Phase 3 — Automation
Tools, actions, integrations, and workflow automation.

### Phase 4 — Business Platform
Billing, analytics, team management, permissions, and advanced administration.

### Phase 5 — Scale
Performance optimization, security hardening, integrations, and international expansion.

## 11. Success Criteria

The MVP will be considered successful when a real business user can:

1. Create an account.
2. Create an AI Employee.
3. Define its role and instructions.
4. Add business knowledge.
5. Test the AI Employee through conversation.
6. Deploy it to a website.
7. Monitor its basic activity.

# AgentPilot AI — Project Charter

## 1. Project Overview

AgentPilot AI is an AI Workforce SaaS platform designed to help businesses create, configure, deploy, and manage intelligent AI employees.

AI employees can support customers, use business knowledge, perform defined tasks, and operate according to business rules and permissions.

---

## 2. Vision

To build a trusted global AI Workforce platform that enables businesses to deploy intelligent digital employees without requiring advanced technical expertise.

---

## 3. Mission

Our mission is to make AI automation accessible to businesses of all sizes by providing simple, reliable, and powerful tools for creating, managing, and deploying AI employees.

---

## 4. Problem Statement

Businesses spend significant amounts of time and money handling repetitive customer interactions, information requests, internal processes, and routine operational tasks.

Many existing AI solutions can be powerful but may require technical knowledge, complex configuration, or multiple disconnected services.

AgentPilot AI aims to provide a unified workspace where businesses can create, configure, deploy, and manage AI employees from one platform.

---

## 5. Target Users

AgentPilot AI is initially designed for:

- Small and medium-sized businesses
- E-commerce businesses
- Startups
- Marketing agencies
- Educational organizations
- Service businesses
- Independent professionals

---

## 6. Core Product Concept

The core product is the **AI Employee**.

Each AI Employee should have:

- A defined role
- A personality and communication style
- Business knowledge
- Instructions and policies
- Authorized tools
- Defined tasks
- Permission boundaries
- Performance monitoring

---

## 7. Initial MVP

The first Minimum Viable Product (MVP) will focus on:

- User registration and authentication
- Business workspace
- AI Employee creation
- AI Employee configuration
- Knowledge base
- Document upload
- AI conversation
- Website chat widget
- Basic analytics
- Employee and workspace management

---

## 8. Product Principles

AgentPilot AI will be designed around the following principles:

1. Simplicity
2. Reliability
3. Security
4. Transparency
5. Human control
6. Scalability
7. Excellent user experience

---

## 9. Technology Direction

The initial technology direction includes:

- Next.js
- React
- Node.js
- PostgreSQL
- AI API integration
- GitHub
- Cloud deployment

Technology choices may evolve during development based on technical and business requirements.

---

## 10. Development Phases

### Phase 1 — Foundation

Project documentation, repository structure, product requirements, and system architecture.

### Phase 2 — MVP

Core AI Employee creation, knowledge management, conversations, and workspace management.

### Phase 3 — Automation

Tools, actions, integrations, and workflow automation.

### Phase 4 — Business Platform

Billing, analytics, team management, permissions, and advanced administration.

### Phase 5 — Scale

Performance optimization, security hardening, integrations, reliability improvements, and international expansion.

---

## 11. Success Criteria

The MVP will be considered successful when a real business user can:

1. Create an account.
2. Create an AI Employee.
3. Define its role and instructions.
4. Add business knowledge.
5. Test the AI Employee through conversation.
6. Deploy it to a website.
7. Monitor its basic activity.

---

## 12. Project Status

**Current Status:** Foundation and Architecture

**Version:** 0.1.0

---

## 13. Long-Term Goal

AgentPilot AI aims to evolve from an AI assistant platform into a complete AI Workforce operating system where businesses can create, manage, supervise, and scale multiple specialized AI employees.

The long-term platform may support customer service, sales, marketing, operations, research, administration, and other business workflows.

# AgentPilot AI — Foundation

## 1. Product Identity

**Product Name:** AgentPilot AI

**Category:** AI Workforce Platform

**Product Type:** SaaS

**Core Concept:** AI Employees

---

## 2. Product Vision

AgentPilot AI aims to become a trusted global platform for creating, deploying, and managing intelligent AI employees.

The platform will allow businesses to build specialized AI employees that can understand business knowledge, communicate with customers, perform approved tasks, and operate within clearly defined permissions.

---

## 3. The Problem

Businesses face repetitive work across customer support, sales, administration, research, and daily operations.

Traditional automation tools often require complex configuration, technical expertise, or multiple disconnected systems.

Businesses need a simpler way to deploy AI that is useful, controllable, and aligned with their own operations.

---

## 4. Our Solution

AgentPilot AI provides a unified workspace where a business can:

1. Create an AI Employee.
2. Define its role.
3. Give it business knowledge.
4. Define its behavior and instructions.
5. Connect approved tools.
6. Define permissions and boundaries.
7. Deploy the AI Employee.
8. Monitor its activity and performance.

---

## 5. The AI Employee Model

Every AI Employee is built from several components:

### Identity
Who the AI Employee is and what role it represents.

### Knowledge
Information provided by the business.

### Instructions
Rules that define how the AI Employee should behave.

### Tools
Actions and external services the AI Employee is allowed to use.

### Permissions
What the AI Employee is allowed and not allowed to do.

### Memory
Relevant information that may be retained according to the platform's policies.

### Monitoring
Logs, analytics, and performance information used to supervise the AI Employee.

---

## 6. Initial AI Employee Roles

The platform may initially support roles such as:

- Customer Support Employee
- Sales Employee
- Marketing Employee
- Research Employee
- Administrative Employee

The role system will be designed to expand as the platform develops.

---

## 7. Core Product Experience

The primary user journey should be:

**Create → Configure → Teach → Test → Deploy → Monitor → Improve**

The experience should remain simple enough for a non-technical business owner.

---

## 8. MVP Scope

The first release will focus on:

- Authentication
- Business workspace
- AI Employee creation
- AI Employee configuration
- Knowledge base
- Document ingestion
- AI chat
- Website deployment
- Basic analytics
- Basic permissions
- Activity monitoring

Features outside this scope will be evaluated after the MVP is validated.

---

## 9. Product Principles

AgentPilot AI will prioritize:

- Simplicity
- Reliability
- Security
- Human control
- Transparency
- Privacy
- Scalability
- Excellent user experience

---

## 10. Long-Term Direction

AgentPilot AI may evolve into an AI Workforce operating system where businesses can manage multiple specialized AI employees from a single platform.

The long-term platform may support:

- Customer service
- Sales
- Marketing
- Research
- Administration
- Operations
- Business workflows
- Third-party integrations

---

## 11. Success Definition

The first version will be successful when a real business can create an AI Employee, provide its knowledge and instructions, test its behavior, deploy it to customers, and monitor its activity without requiring advanced technical knowledge.
